# Information needed for each genuine case study

No approved completed-project records or client-approved job photographs have
been supplied to this repository. Illustrative room photographs must retain
their illustration label and must not be relabelled as completed jobs.

For each proposed case study record:

- Internal source record identifying the completed instruction.
- The business that performed the work and permission to attribute it to HPSG.
- Service, neighbourhood and postcode district (not a private street address).
- Work actually completed, relevant constraints and the documented solution.
- Actual photographs, ownership/licence and the client's publication permission.
- Owner approval of the final description and each image.

The `publication` field on a project requires the source record, completed
status, owner approval and photo consent. Every descriptive field must be free
of unresolved draft markers. Missing approval keeps the route unpublished.
Never copy a sister company's project into HPSG without confirming attribution.
